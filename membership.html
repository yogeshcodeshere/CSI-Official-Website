WHAT IS CSI MEMBERSHIP?

The Computer Society of India (CSI) is the premier professional body for IT specialists, researchers, and students across India. Established in 1965, CSI fosters the exchange of cutting-edge knowledge, innovative research, and technical camaraderie.

At SIES GST, the CSI Student Chapter represents an elite hub where ambitious engineers transform classroom theory into high-impact industry applications. Becoming a registered CSI member unlocks unparalleled access to our technical ecosystem.

National Accreditation

Recognized student chapter affiliation backed by the Computer Society of India national council.

Industry & Project Bridge

Hands-on development sprints in AI, Cloud, Cybersecurity, and Web3 guided by seasoned practitioners.

Elite Alumni & Mentor Network

Direct peer connections with seniors, alumni placed at top tech enterprises, and faculty experts.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers about membership enrollment, benefits activation, and chapter access.

Who is eligible to join CSI SIESGST?

Any student enrolled at SIES Graduate School of Technology from any academic year (FE, SE, TE, BE) and any engineering discipline (CE, IT, ECS, AIML, IOT, ME) is eligible to join. We celebrate cross-disciplinary collaboration.

How soon do my membership privileges activate?

Your membership privileges activate immediately upon confirmation by the student council. You will receive your official member registration ID and gain instant entry to our members-only portal and event discounts.

How do I redeem discounts on workshops and hackathons?

Whenever registration opens for events such as Innovations, Enigma, or technical bootcamps, members enter their verified CSI Member ID at checkout to automatically apply complimentary access or steep member-only pricing.

Can members contribute to The Megabyte magazine?

Yes! All active CSI members receive exclusive call-for-papers invitations to submit articles, technical breakdowns, interviews, and project showcases to the editorial committee of "The Megabyte".

Need further assistance?
