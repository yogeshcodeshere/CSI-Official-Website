// 01 • WHO ARE WE

India’s Largest Tech Network,
Powered by Students

Founded in 1965, the Computer Society of India (CSI) is the nation’s pioneer professional body dedicated to advancing computer engineering, data science, and IT research.

At SIES Graduate School of Technology, our Student Chapter transforms this national legacy into an electric campus community. We are an elected guild of student developers, designers, open-source hackers, and tech leads committed to bridging the gap between theoretical classroom knowledge and industry-scale engineering.
