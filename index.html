// WHO ARE WE

India’s Largest Tech Network,
Powered by Students

Founded in 1965, the Computer Society of India (CSI) is the nation’s pioneer professional body dedicated to advancing computer engineering, data science, and IT research.

At SIES Graduate School of Technology, our Student Chapter transforms this national legacy into an electric campus community. We are an elected guild of student developers, designers, open-source hackers, and tech leads committed to bridging the gap between theoretical classroom knowledge and industry-scale engineering.

INNOVATION 2026

INNOVATION 2026 was a national-level project presentation competition by CSI SIESGST, providing a platform for students to showcase innovative projects, research, prototypes, and ideas. The event celebrated creativity, technology, and problem-solving while connecting student innovation with real-world opportunities.

ENIGMA 4.0: CHRONIX

ENIGMA 4.0 – CHRONIX was a national-level web development hackathon by CSI SIESGST, bringing together innovators to build impactful technology solutions. With the theme “One Event. Infinite Realities.”, participants explored real-world challenges across FinTech and HealthTech through ideation, prototyping, and pitching.

5 Photos

SYNAPSE 1.0

SYNAPSE 1.0 was an immersive job-simulation experience where participants stepped into the roles of an AI System Investigator and Product Strategist. Teams analysed a real-world AI system failure, identified its root cause, and redesigned the system through critical thinking, analysis, and decision-making.
